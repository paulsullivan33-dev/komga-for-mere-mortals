The lab — one release jar, one generated series, nobody else's files
====================================================================

The lab is Komga as its project ships it: the
official release jar, komga-1.30.0.jar from the
1.30.0 release at github.com/gotson/komga
(tags carry no leading "v"), verified against
the release's checksums_sha256.txt (SHA-256
cac820a2...d8ff3) and run on OpenJDK 21.0.12.1.
The server bound 127.0.0.1 port 18090 with its
config folder inside the lab scratch; the jar
itself is not in the checkout (127 MB), the
transcripts name it exactly.

  * The fixtures (komga-lab/fixtures/library/):
    one invented series, "Clockwork Orchard",
    drawn by make_fixtures.py with Pillow —
    simple panels, shapes, and printed text,
    no copied art and nobody real. Three CBZ
    issues (6, 5, and 4 pages of 800x1100 PNG),
    plus "Clockwork Orchard 004.cbz", which is
    plain text in a .cbz name, kept in every
    count. add-issue-005.py later files a
    3-page issue 005 and rebuilds 003 with a
    ComicInfo.xml inside (same pages), so the
    checkout's final fixture state is the
    end-of-lab state: 5 files, 4 readable.

  * The accounts are the lab's own inventions:
    keeper@clockwork.example (claimed
    administrator) and reader@clockwork.example
    (a role-less second user), passwords in the
    transcripts because the lab is disposable
    and loopback-only.

  * Every claim's evidence
    (komga-lab/transcripts/): t00 version and
    checksum; t01 the fixture generation
    record the server is later checked
    against; t02 the startup banner, version
    line, 95 migrations, the port line; t03
    the first user — two dead ends (401 on
    POST /api/v1/users, 404 on
    /api/v1/users/me) kept on record, then the
    claim flow the web interface itself makes;
    t04 the library record with its defaults
    and the scan logs; t05 the series and its
    books with page counts and the odd file's
    UNSUPPORTED verdict; t06 the page list and
    a page read back byte-identical; t07 the
    OPDS catalog, series feed, a byte-identical
    download, and the PSE page stream; t08
    search and read progress; t09 the second
    user and the roles' 403s; t10 the new
    issue and the ComicInfo rescan; t11
    thumbnail, OPDS 2, and the settings door.

  * Two accidents, kept on record: the jar
    download was reported complete by the
    shell while the transfer was still
    running; the lab noticed because the file
    kept growing, waited, and verified size
    and SHA-256 before use. And the guide's
    first-user and v1/v2 dead ends (t03, t09)
    are preserved in the transcripts rather
    than edited into a tidy fiction — they are
    how the chapters know what they know.
